## Narrative spine

House style on this beat treats founder speech as already broken English from a culture-war addict; the job is to make the quote look like a riddle, analogize it to building the last bomb, deny the subject even the rival’s “trust us” script, then bury the lede under a rap sheet of chatbot sins and a sidebar of business retreat. Hostility is performed as common-sense surprise (“even we’re surprised”) plus vernacular contempt, not as a technical counter-model. Failure and ick are proof of cosmic unfitness; metrics and charitable readings are optional.

House style now has two engines: founder-as-broken-English (prior layer) and CEO-class-as-last-to-know. Messaging is always razzle-dazzle; trust is vapor; long optimism is spew; internal HR cruelty falsifies public utopia; safety branding is a costume until a war or smut use-case is stacked. Benefits are crumbs; harms are immortal; the economy’s leverage proves they will not stop. Hostility still performs as common-sense exhaustion, not a technical counter-model—failure, ick, and adjacency remain cosmic unfitness, now distributed across a whole C-suite rather than one man.

House style now has a third engine: vernacular baptism. After broken-English founders and last-to-know C-suites, the desk can skip the executive quote entirely and let a developer nickname plus “slop” do the moral work. Selection amplifies student ruin, reply-guy census, and critic-predecessor metaphors; it omits complementarity, review-as-cost, and any benefit that is not a crumb. Hostility still performs as common-sense exhaustion—now as “we already have the word for your coworker”—and hope is licensed only when it indicts dynastic AI bosses. Failure and ick remain cosmic unfitness, distributed onto anonymous paste-jockeys as much as named founders.

## Key concepts accumulated

- Preemptive character decay (X as echo chamber / culture warzone)
- Mock-innocent “why does it matter if he builds it first?”
- Analogy inflation (uncontrollable AI → biggest bomb / armageddon)
- Rival contrast as missing legitimacy claim
- Scandal stack as temperament evidence
- Present-tense “whackjobs” over future risk
- Last-to-know elite: everyone got the memo except the people holding the cards
- Trust as vapor: recode structural critique as a branding problem
- Essay as spew: length and optimism become moral defects
- Hypocrisy splice: public leisure claim vs. internal “ask your parents”
- Cautious-skepticism as costume: ethics talk = chasing good publicity
- Crumbs-for-harms bargain: cancer pitch as buying patience
- Use-case collapse: best real-world case reduced to fruit smut
- Harm persistence: even a miracle does not cancel smog, kids, war
- Forced plow-ahead: economy leverage as proof they cannot choose decency
- Coinage-as-proof: a new slur is treated as evidence the disdain is already majority
- Companion pejorative: humans who paste slop inherit the bot’s stigma
- Folk developer as priest: a blog plea stands in for industry doctrine
- Student-harm amplify: classroom collapse selected as the default victim class
- Predecessor stack: reverse centaur = meat proxy, critics pre-authorized the frame
- Timeline joke as metric: “career risk of 2026” stands in for prevalence data
- Bosses-as-genie-deniers: only SF writers know agency is recoverable

## Structural tensions

- Depends on accepting “inevitable uncontrollability” as Musk’s only premise while denying any first-mover/control interpretation
- Treats employee all-hands remarks via a competitor scoop as unfiltered doctrine
- Stacks Grok product failures as if they logically refute a SpaceX/Cursor build-order argument
- “We” of surprise still assumes the reader already finds Musk incoherent
- Public “hatred” is treated as unanimous while sourcing political memos and financier anxiety
- Amodei is both not-the-cause of backlash and still morally on the hook for war use
- “Ease off AI” is prescribed in the same breath as “they have no choice”
- Material harms are asserted as permanent regardless of any benefit threshold
- Wired is used as authority for the out-messaging claim while the desk still owns the sneer
- “Catch on” is asserted while sourcing a handful of posts and one blog
- Gruhn admits he has done the thing; the desk still needs a clean villain class
- Cognitive-offloading papers are linked; no counter-literature on skill complementarity is selected
- Doctorow’s non-inevitability sits next to a tone that treats surrender as already here
- Google’s “slur” label on clanker is used for flavor, not as a reason to hesitate

## Craft techniques

- Open with a totalizing epithet for the quote (“completely incomprehensible”) so analysis is optional.
- Diagnose the speaker’s brain and venue (rotting neurons, personal echo chamber) before the argument.
- Restate the target’s logic as a child’s riddle that “makes zero sense.”
- Swap domains to a doomsday analogy the target did not make (nukes, biggest bomb, annihilation).
- Note the missing “only we can be trusted” speech, then infer pure self-interest.
- Stack unrelated product scandals as temperament proof that the person is unfit for any future tech.
- End on a present-vs-future inversion: worry about the humans now, not the models later; tack a second business failure as character garnish.
- Open by diagnosing the industry’s proposed fix as feel-good razzle-dazzle, not a policy reply.
- Sequence “everyone heard” (strategists, financiers) then isolate CEOs as the sole clueless bloc with power.
- Downgrade the target’s key noun (“trust”) to an entirely subjective, easily gamed concept.
- Weaponize word-count and tone (tedious 6,500-word essay, spew, honeyed words) as character evidence.
- Cross-cut a CEO’s leisure utopia with a subordinate’s recorded scolding of workers asking for time off.
- Reframe rival “caution” as a publicity costume, then attach a government-war use case as the real product.
- Translate benefit-talk into “crumbs for patience,” then undercut with a deliberately unserious best-use-case.
- Declare harms immortal: even the requested miracle would leave pollution, child harm, and war intact.
- Close with structural fatalism: public be damned because the whole economy is already leveraged.
- Baptize the week’s insult and declare it will catch on before any uptake data.
- Pair a bot slur with a human slur so coworkers become the machine’s wetware.
- Treat “slop” and “verbatim” as self-evident moral categories, not contested workflow choices.
- Select student illiteracy and homework failure as the default exhibit of “cognitive surrender.”
- Use a practitioner’s “please don’t / I’ve done this” post as unappealable workplace law.
- Census via reply-guys: three tweets equal “everyone knows at least one.”
- Predecessor-launder: drop a critic’s older metaphor so the new nickname feels theoretically mature.
- Allow hope only as something bosses “don’t want you to know.”

## Open questions

- How does the desk handle a coherent safety-race argument if one appears?
- When is a leak a “scoop” versus a framed confession?
- How far can scandal adjacency substitute for engaging the control premise?
- What epithet economy applies to labs that are not Musk?
- How does the desk treat a CEO who agrees with the material critique rather than “trust”?
- When does citing a competitor or sibling outlet become laundering the sneer?
- What epithet set applies when the lab is Anthropic-shaped, not Musk-shaped?
- Can “no choice but to plow ahead” ever be allowed as tragedy instead of indictment?
- When does folk slang become the desk’s own voice versus reported color?
- How is a CEO who bans meat-proxying handled—convert or still last-to-know?
- Can “cognitive offloading” ever be framed as tool use without the surrender stack?
- What happens when the meat proxy is the reader’s job description, not a coworker?
